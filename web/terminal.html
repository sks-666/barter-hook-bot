    COMMANDS

    BTC · BTCUSDTLoad a coin (USD-M perpetual). Add an interval or a panel: ETH 4H, SOL BOOK.
    1M 5M 15M 1H 4H 1D 1WChart interval (1M = one minute).
    ARB · MOV · POS · LOGJump to the basis monitor, movers, positions or event log.
    CHART · BOOK · TAPE · SENTJump to the chart, order book, trades or sentiment.
    WHALE · LIQ · FLOWBig trades ($50K+, more on busy coins), liquidations across the market, and each coin's whale buying vs selling. FLOW 4H (or 1S 5S 15S 30S 1M 5M 15M 30M 1H 1D 1W) sets the flow timeframe.
    SENTWhales vs crowd: the top traders' long share against all accounts', with taker flow and open interest. SENT 4H (or 5M 15M 30M 1H 2H 6H 12H 1D 1W) sets one row's step.
    ALERT BTC > 82000Price alert on the mark price (also ALERT BTC 82000: above or below is picked from the price now). Alt+click the chart to set one at that price.
    ALERT FUND BTC > 0.05Funding alert, in percent.
    ALERT WHALE [SOL] [500K]Every whale order of that size (default $1M) on one coin or any coin; at most one message a minute per alert.
    ALERT LIQ [BTC] [1M]Liquidations of that size or more.
    ALERTS · UNALERT 3Show the alerts panel; remove alert #3. Alerts go to Telegram when it is set up, and always show here.
    DESKResearch gates (walk-forward, deflated Sharpe, crash window, noise test…) and the paper→live ladder for the trend, swing and smc strategies. Refresh with python desk.py research.
    WHLChart: whale orders (circles, sized by value) and liquidations (triangles) on/off.
    VWAP · EMA · CVD · LVLChart overlays on/off: session VWAP, EMA 9/21, buy−sell volume delta, day open/high/low and yesterday's high/low.
    MOV G / L / VMovers: gainers, losers, or volume.
    DARK · LIGHT · AUTOTheme.
