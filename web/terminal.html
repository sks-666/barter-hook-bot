    COMMANDS

    BTC · BTCUSDTLoad a coin (USD-M perpetual). Add an interval or a panel: ETH 4H, SOL BOOK.
    1M 5M 15M 1H 4H 1D 1WChart interval (1M = one minute).
    ARB · MOV · POS · LOGJump to the basis monitor, movers, positions or event log.
    CHART · BOOK · TAPE · SENTJump to the chart, order book, trades or sentiment.
    WHALE · LIQ · FLOWBig trades ($50K+, more on busy coins), liquidations across the market, and each coin's whale buying vs selling. FLOW 4H (or 1S 5S 15S 30S 1M 5M 15M 30M 1H 1D 1W) sets the flow timeframe.
    SIGNALS · SIGNALS 4HThe Signals panel: one long / short idea per coin from whale flow, whales vs crowd, the futures curve, macro and news, with entry, stop, target and why. SIGNALS 4H (or 1M 5M 15M 30M 1H 1D 1W) sets its timeframe.
    SENTWhales vs crowd: the top traders' long share against all accounts', with taker flow and open interest. SENT 4H (or 5M 15M 30M 1H 2H 6H 12H 1D 1W) sets one row's step.
    ALERT BTC > 82000Price alert on the mark price (also ALERT BTC 82000: above or below is picked from the price now). Alt+click the chart to set one at that price.
    ALERT FUND BTC > 0.05Funding alert, in percent.
    ALERT WHALE [SOL] [500K]Every whale order of that size (default $1M) on one coin or any coin; at most one message a minute per alert.
    ALERT LIQ [BTC] [1M]Liquidations of that size or more.
    ALERTS · UNALERT 3Show the alerts panel; remove alert #3. Alerts go to Telegram when it is set up, and always show here.
    DESKResearch gates (walk-forward, deflated Sharpe, crash window, noise test…) and the paper→live ladder for the trend, swing and smc strategies. Refresh with python desk.py research.
    CURVE · YC · GCFutures curve, the crypto version of YC/GC: the perpetual and the quarterly futures, how far each trades above spot (the index), that per year, and the history of each spread plus the funding rate. CURVE 4H (or 1H 12H 1D) sets the history step. Only BTC and ETH have quarterlies; other coins show the perpetual.
    COMP BTC ETH SOLCompare 2 to 4 coins side by side: returns over 1D/1W/1M/3M/6M, volatility, drawdown, volume, funding, open interest, and correlation and beta to the first coin. COMP alone shows the panel.
    EXPORT [BTC ETH] [1D] [5Y]Download daily (or 1W 4H 1H) candles as one CSV file, up to 5 years, for the coins named or the compare list. Real Binance USD-M perpetual prices.
    ECO · MACRO · NEWSMacro & news: inflation, unemployment, the Fed rate, Treasury yields, the yield curve, the dollar, VIX, gold, oil, S&P 500 and bitcoin, each with a plain read; consumer discretionary (XLY) vs utilities (XLU); how stocks and bitcoin have reacted to yields; and live headlines (Fed, CNBC economy, CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt). ECO 3M (or 1W 1M 6M 1Y) sets the look-back. NEWS FED (or MACRO CRYPTO INFLATION JOBS GROWTH REGULATION HACK, or a coin like NEWS SOL) filters the headlines. It reads what has happened; it does not forecast.
    RISKPosition size calculator: capital, risk % per trade and stop give the size, leverage, liquidation price, 1R/2R/3R targets and a daily loss limit. It places nothing.
    EMA50 · RSI · SIGChart: EMA 50, an RSI 14 pane, and the EMA50 + RSI swing signals on/off. Long when a candle closes above EMA50 and RSI crosses back up through 40; exit when RSI falls back under 70 or a close is below EMA50. Short is the mirror (below EMA50, RSI down through 60; exit up through 30 or a close above EMA50). Closed candles only; past results are before fees and say nothing about the next trade.
    WHLChart: whale orders (circles, sized by value) and liquidations (triangles) on/off.
    VWAP · EMA · CVD · LVLChart overlays on/off: session VWAP, EMA 9/21, buy−sell volume delta, day open/high/low and yesterday's high/low.
    MOV G / L / VMovers: gainers, losers, or volume.
